# Plan Coverage Summary

## Available Plans

The Wellbeing program offers two plan tiers: **Wellbeing Gold** and
**Wellbeing Silver**. Your plan tier was selected during onboarding or the
most recent open enrollment period and can be viewed in the portal under
"My Plan."

## Wellbeing Gold Coverage

- **Mental Health**: Covered at $20 copay per visit, up to $1,500 per year.
  Includes therapy, counseling, and psychiatric visits with in-network
  providers.
- **Dental**: Preventive care (cleanings, checkups) covered at $0 copay.
  Procedures such as fillings or root canals are covered at 80%, up to
  $2,000 per year.
- **Wellness Reimbursement**: Up to $300 per year for gym memberships,
  fitness classes, or approved wellness apps. Submit receipts as you would
  any other claim.
- **Vision**: Not covered under Gold.

## Wellbeing Silver Coverage

- **Mental Health**: Covered at $35 copay per visit, up to $800 per year.
- **Dental**: Not covered.
- **Wellness Reimbursement**: Up to $150 per year.
- **Vision**: Not covered.

## Dependents

Employees may add a spouse and/or children as dependents on their plan
during open enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event
(marriage, birth, adoption). Dependents share the same coverage tier and
annual limits as the primary plan holder, but each dependent has their own
separate annual limit — a spouse's mental health visits, for example, do
not count against the employee's own $1,500 limit.

## In-Network vs. Out-of-Network

Both plans only cover in-network providers. You can search for in-network
providers in the Wellbeing portal under "Find a Provider." Out-of-network
care is not reimbursed under either plan, regardless of the type of service.

## Changing Plans

Plan tier changes can only be made during the annual open enrollment period
(typically in November) or within 30 days of a qualifying life event. Mid-year
changes outside these windows are not permitted.
