# Claims Filing Process

## Overview

This guide explains how employees submit a claim for reimbursement under the
Wellbeing insurance program, and what happens after submission.

## How to File a Claim

1. Log in to the Wellbeing portal and select "Submit a New Claim."
2. Choose the claim type: Medical, Dental, Mental Health, or Wellness Reimbursement.
3. Upload an itemized receipt or invoice from your provider. A credit card
   statement alone is not sufficient — the receipt must show the service
   provided, the date, and the amount charged.
4. Enter the date of service and a short description of the treatment or
   purchase.
5. Submit the claim. You will receive a confirmation email with a claim ID
   (format CLM-XXXX) that you can use to track its status.

## Claim Statuses

- **SUBMITTED** — Received, not yet reviewed.
- **IN_REVIEW** — A claims analyst is verifying the documentation. This is
  the most common reason a claim appears "stuck" — usually the analyst is
  waiting on additional documentation such as an itemized receipt.
- **APPROVED** — The claim has been approved for payment. Approved claims
  are paid out in the next payroll cycle, which runs on the last business
  day of each month.
- **REJECTED** — The claim was denied. A rejection always includes a reason,
  such as the service not being covered under your plan, or the provider
  being out-of-network.
- **PAID** — Payment has been issued.

## Typical Timeline

Most claims are reviewed within 5-7 business days of submission. Claims
missing documentation will remain in IN_REVIEW status until the employee
uploads what's requested — the system does not automatically reject
incomplete claims, but they also will not progress until resolved.

## Appealing a Rejected Claim

If you believe a claim was rejected in error, you can file an appeal within
30 days of the rejection date through the portal's "Appeal a Claim" option.
Appeals are reviewed by a different analyst than the original decision and
typically take 10 business days.

## Common Reasons for Rejection

- Provider is out-of-network and the plan does not cover out-of-network care.
- The service is explicitly excluded from the plan (see the Plan Coverage
  Summary for what's included).
- Missing or illegible documentation.
- The claim was submitted more than 90 days after the date of service.
