# Wellness Program FAQ

## What counts as a wellness expense?

Gym memberships, fitness class packages (yoga, spin, martial arts, etc.),
and a small list of approved wellness apps (currently: Calm, Headspace, and
MyFitnessPal premium subscriptions) all qualify. Home gym equipment,
supplements, and nutrition coaching are not currently eligible.

## How do I submit a wellness reimbursement claim?

The same way as any other claim — select "Wellness Reimbursement" as the
claim type when filing, and attach a receipt showing the merchant, date, and
amount. See the Claims Filing Process guide for the full steps.

## Is the wellness reimbursement a monthly or annual limit?

It's an annual limit ($300 for Gold, $150 for Silver), not monthly. You can
submit multiple smaller claims throughout the year until you hit your limit,
or one larger claim if your annual membership is paid upfront.

## Does the wellness reimbursement roll over to next year?

No. Unused wellness reimbursement does not carry over — it resets to the
full annual amount at the start of each calendar year.

## Can I use the wellness reimbursement for a family member's gym membership?

Only if that family member is enrolled as a dependent on your plan, and the
membership is in their name with its own limit — it does not share your
personal $300/$150 allowance.

## I was told about a "step challenge" program — is that part of this?

The quarterly step challenge is a separate engagement program run by HR and
is not tied to claims or reimbursement. Questions about the step challenge
should go to the HR wellness team directly, not through the claims process.

## My wellness claim was rejected — why?

The most common reasons are: the expense type isn't on the approved list
(see the first question above), the receipt didn't clearly show the
merchant name, or the annual limit was already reached for the year. Check
your claim's status note in the portal for the specific reason.
